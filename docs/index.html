rentready

Is your rental ready for England's landlord database? From 15 December 2026 every private landlord in England registers each property on Register your rental property (Renters' Rights Act), region by region, at £65 a property a year, uploading the gas safety record, EICR and EPC. No active entry, no possession order; penalties to £7,000.

rentready tells you your region's window and deadline, whether your certificates will still be in date, what to book, what to upload and what it costs. Free, zero dependencies, one Python file. Also an MCP server, so your own Claude or ChatGPT agent can run it.

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/genzagents/rentready/main/rentready/__init__.py | python3 - check "B3 2TA" --gas=2026-02-01 --eicr=2023-06-01 --epc=2017-05-01 --epc-rating=D
