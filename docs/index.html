rentready

Is your rental ready for England's landlord database? From 15 December 2026 every private landlord in England registers each property on Register your rental property (Renters' Rights Act), region by region, at £65 a property a year, uploading the gas safety record, EICR and EPC. No active entry, no possession order; penalties to £7,000.

rentready tells you your region's window and deadline, whether your certificates will still be in date, what to book, what to upload and what it costs. Free, zero dependencies, one Python file. Also an MCP server, so your own Claude or ChatGPT agent can run it.

Several properties, and the dates in your calendar

New in 0.2.0. Put your properties in a CSV (name, postcode, gas, no_gas, eicr, epc, epc_rating) and get one table with every warning, the total fee and the earliest deadline. Then write an .ics file with the day your region's window opens, the deadline and every gas, EICR and EPC expiry, each with reminders 60 and 14 days before, and import it into Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. Nothing is stored anywhere but your own calendar.

rentready portfolio properties.csv
rentready calendar properties.csv --out=rentready.ics

An email when something needs action (0.3.0)

Put properties.csv in a private GitHub repo and add one workflow file. On the first of every month rentready watch re-checks every property, keeps one issue up to date with the whole table, and comments on it only when a gas safety record, EICR or EPC comes within 60 days of expiring, your region's window opens, a deadline nears or passes, or a property stops being ready. GitHub emails you the comment. Free, no server, no account beyond GitHub. Issue #1 watches the example portfolio.

steps:
  - uses: actions/checkout@v4
  - uses: genzagents/rentready@v0.4.0
    with:
      portfolio: properties.csv

Full template: docs/watch.yml. Or from cron on your own machine: rentready watch properties.csv --state=state.json.

Want the hosted monthly watch?

The paid step for landlords who do not want a GitHub repo: pay once a year, paste your properties into the checkout, and get the first report within the hour, an email the moment something needs action, and a short digest once a month. Nothing to install or keep. £12 a year for up to 10 properties, not £15 a month. Every email is reproducible: the inputs and date are stored beside it and rentready hosted replay re-runs it. Letting agents can run the same watch for every landlord they manage from one file (how).
