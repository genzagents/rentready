rentready

Is your rental ready for England's landlord database? From 15 December 2026 every private landlord in England registers each let property on Register your rental property (Renters' Rights Act), region by region, at £65 a property a year, uploading the gas safety record, EICR and EPC. Unoccupied properties need not be registered yet, and supported exempt accommodation is excluded (official guidance). No active entry, no possession order, except on Ground 7A or Ground 14 (section 90). Penalties up to £7,000 for a breach (section 91) and up to £40,000 for the offences in section 92: false information, continuing a breach after a penalty, or a repeat within five years.

rentready tells you your region's window and deadline, whether your certificates will still be in date, what to book, what to upload and what it costs. Free. Check right here in the browser, or from the terminal: zero dependencies, one Python file, and an MCP server so your own Claude or ChatGPT agent can run it.

Your deadline: West Midlands · East of England · East Midlands · South East · Yorkshire and The Humber · North West · North East · London · South West.

Guides: deadlines by region, the £65 fee, what to upload, gas, EICR and EPC, penalties, letting agents and a checklist.

Several properties, and the dates in your calendar

New in 0.2.0. Put your properties in a CSV (name, postcode, gas, no_gas, eicr, epc, epc_rating) and get one table with every warning, the total fee and the earliest deadline. Then write an .ics file with the day your region's window opens, the deadline and every gas, EICR and EPC expiry, each with reminders 60 and 14 days before, and import it into Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. Nothing is stored anywhere but your own calendar.

rentready portfolio properties.csv

The registration pack (in the browser from 0.7.0)

Press Show my registration pack after a check above, print it or save it. Every question the service asks, in its own words, for you once and for each property: dwelling, tenancy, rent, gas, EICR and EPC. Answers come pre-filled from your CSV, each gap is marked GATHER with the column to add, and each certificate gets a file name to scan it as and a state: ready, renew soon, or renew before you upload. A property is ready to register only when every answer and file is in hand.

rentready pack properties.csv --out=pack.md

An email when something needs action (0.3.0)

Put properties.csv in a private GitHub repo and add one workflow file. On the first of every month rentready watch re-checks every property, keeps one issue up to date with the whole table, and comments on it only when a gas safety record, EICR or EPC comes within 60 days of expiring, your region's window opens, a deadline nears or passes, or a property stops being ready. GitHub emails you the comment. Free, no server, no account beyond GitHub. Issue #1 watches the example portfolio.

steps:
  - uses: actions/checkout@v4
  - uses: genzagents/rentready@v0.7.1
    with:
      portfolio: properties.csv

Full template: docs/watch.yml. Or from cron on your own machine: rentready watch properties.csv --state=state.json.
