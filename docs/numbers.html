How we count

  • Revenue and paying landlords: money actually received for the reminder service (£12 a year for up to 10 properties). No projections and no annualised figures.
  • Reminder sign-ups: landlords who asked for the monthly certificate and deadline email.
  • Pages in Google's index: rentready pages that a site: search on Google returns.
  • AI answers that cite us: each week we ask the same 15 landlord database questions and count the answers that link to a rentready page. This week Google showed an AI Overview for 15 of them. The sources it cited most were nrla.org.uk (9), theindependentlandlord.com (5), gov.uk (5), housinghub.campaign.gov.uk (5), osborneslaw.com (4).
  • GitHub visitors: unique people who opened the rentready or citable code on GitHub in the last 14 days, from GitHub's own traffic figures. The checker itself has no analytics and no cookies, so we do not count its visitors yet.
