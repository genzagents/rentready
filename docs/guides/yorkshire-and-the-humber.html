Landlord database registration in Yorkshire and the Humber: deadline 14 July 2027

Written by GenZAgents, reviewed 28 October 2026. Sources below.

If your rental is in Yorkshire and the Humber, you must register it on the government's Register your rental property service (the landlord database, or PRS Database) by 14 July 2027. The requirement for Yorkshire and the Humber starts on 15 April 2027; the service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier. The region goes by the property's address, not yours. Yorkshire and the Humber covers Leeds, Sheffield, Bradford, Hull, York, Huddersfield, Doncaster and Wakefield.

Councils in Yorkshire and the Humber and their licensing schemes

Yorkshire and the Humber has 15 local councils that act as the local housing authority (ONS, May 2026). The registration service asks whether each property needs an HMO, additional or selective licence, and for the licence numbers (draft Schedule 3, paragraph 10), so check your council first. Mandatory HMO licensing applies everywhere; these schemes are on top.

On 28 October 2026 we found 5 councils in Yorkshire and the Humber with selective licensing and 1 with additional HMO licensing, in force or designated to start.

Doncaster

  • Selective licensing (in force): Parts: Hexthorpe. Council page

Leeds

  • Selective licensing (in force): Parts: East, South and West Leeds designation (parts of Armley, Beeston & Holbeck, Burmantofts & Richmond Hill, Hunslet & Riverside, Gipton & Harehills, Farnley & Wortley wards), until 8 February 2031. Council page

North East Lincolnshire

  • Selective licensing (in force): Parts: East Marsh ward, Grimsby, until 7 April 2031. Council page

North Yorkshire

  • Selective licensing (in force): Parts: Scarborough South (parts of Weaponness and Ramshill ward) and Scarborough Town (parts of Castle, Falsgrave, Stepney and Northstead wards). Scarborough South's designation runs until 30 April 2027; Scarborough Town's until 31 May 2029. Council page

Rotherham

  • Selective licensing (in force): Parts: six areas (Town Centre/Eastwood/Clifton/Boston Castle; Masbrough/Kimberworth; Thurcroft; Dinnington; Brinsworth; Parkgate), until 14 February 2031. Council page

York

  • Additional HMO licensing (in force): Parts: Clifton, Fishergate, Fulford and Heslington, Guildhall, Heworth, Hull Road, Micklegate, Osbaldwick and Derwent wards, until 31 March 2028. Council page

No selective or additional scheme found on the council's own website: Barnsley, Bradford, East Riding of Yorkshire, Hull (Kingston upon Hull), Kirklees, Sheffield and Wakefield. That is not a guarantee: a council can start a scheme at any time, so check before you let.

Not verified: we could not confirm the schemes for Calderdale and North Lincolnshire from the council's own website, so we list nothing for them here. Check with the council.

Before 14 July 2027

£65 a property a year (fee). Upload the gas safety record, EICR and EPC (full list) within 28 days or the entry becomes inactive (draft reg 11(2)(a)); renew anything that lapses before 14 July 2027 first. Up to £7,000 if you miss it (penalties).

Questions

When do landlords in Yorkshire and the Humber have to register?

The requirement starts on 15 April 2027 and the deadline is 14 July 2027. The service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

Which councils in Yorkshire and the Humber have selective licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Doncaster, Leeds, North East Lincolnshire, North Yorkshire and Rotherham. Most schemes cover only some wards, so check the council's map for your street.

Which councils in Yorkshire and the Humber have additional HMO licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: York.
