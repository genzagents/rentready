Landlord database (PRS Database) deadlines by region

Written by GenZAgents, reviewed 28 October 2026. Sources below.

Your deadline to register depends on the region the property is in. Properties in the West Midlands must be registered by 14 March 2027. The last region, the South West, has until 14 November 2027. Everyone can register from 15 December 2026, whatever their region.

How the windows work

Each region's requirement starts on the 15th of a month. The deadline is three months later, on the 14th. The official guidance says that after the deadline, councils in that region can start enforcement.

Registration opens for all of England on 15 December 2026. You do not have to wait for your region's start date. Gov.uk says all landlords actively letting property will need to have registered by 14 November 2027 (gov.uk news, 9 September 2026).

Which region is my property in?

It goes by the property's address, not yours. The official guidance gives the example of a London resident with a Birmingham property, who must meet the West Midlands deadline. rentready finds the region from the property's postcode.

The service covers England only. Wales and Scotland are out of scope.

Properties in more than one region

Each property works to its own region's deadline. A landlord with a flat in the East Midlands and a house in the South West has two deadlines: 14 May 2027 and 14 November 2027.

You can register both at once, from 15 December 2026; the official guidance says you can register properties across several regions at the same time. Renewal is every 12 months from your first entry, and properties added later take the same renewal date (in the draft regulations, regulation 10, and in the official guidance since 29 October 2026). Nobody pays for days before 15 November 2027, so the first payment is £5.34 to £64.82 a property depending on when you register, and a later property pays only up to the shared renewal date (official guidance, 29 October 2026; first payment by region).

Plan backwards from the deadline

A certificate that runs out before your deadline needs renewing first. Work out three dates for each property: the gas safety record (annual), the EICR (at least every five years, sooner if the report says so) and the EPC (ten-yearly). Compare each with your deadline.

Here is an example. A West Midlands property has a gas safety record issued on 1 February 2026. It expires on 1 February 2027, before the 14 March 2027 deadline. So the next gas check needs booking before 1 February 2027.

North East: register by 14 September 2027

The requirement for the North East starts on 15 June 2027 and the deadline is 14 September 2027. The North East covers Newcastle upon Tyne, Sunderland, Middlesbrough, Durham, Gateshead and Darlington.

Has your council told landlords yet?

Landlords in the North East register from 15 June 2027, so on 8 November 2026 we read every council's own landlord pages. 8 of 12 mention the landlord database at all, and none gives the dates (the 15 December 2026 opening or the 14 September 2027 deadline). Councils in the North East and their licensing schemes

The North East has 12 local councils that act as the local housing authority (ONS, May 2026). The registration service asks whether each property needs an HMO, additional or selective licence, and for the licence numbers (draft Schedule 3, paragraph 10), so check your council first. Mandatory HMO licensing applies everywhere; these schemes are on top.

EPCs that run out before 14 September 2027

An EPC lasts ten years, and the service asks for a valid one. By our count of the government's EPC register (full data story), about 39,700 rental EPCs in the North East reach ten years between 1 November 2026 and 14 September 2027, 7,700 of them inside the region's own registration window. Far more follow: 67,600 reach ten years in 2028 to 2030. These are certificates, not homes, and include social lets.

Questions

When is the first landlord database deadline?

14 March 2027, for properties in the West Midlands. The requirement there starts on 15 December 2026.

When is the London deadline?

14 October 2027. The requirement in London starts on 15 July 2027.

Can I register before my region's start date?

Yes. Registration opens for all of England on 15 December 2026, and the official guidance says you can register early.

Does the deadline follow my home address or the property?

The property. The official guidance says deadlines depend on where the property is, not where the landlord lives.
