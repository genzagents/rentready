Landlord database registration in London: deadline 14 October 2027

Written by GenZAgents, reviewed 28 October 2026. Sources below.

If your rental is in London, you must register it on the government's Register your rental property service (the landlord database, or PRS Database) by 14 October 2027. The requirement for London starts on 15 July 2027; the service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier. The region goes by the property's address, not yours. London covers all 32 London boroughs and the City of London.

Councils in London and their licensing schemes

London has 33 local councils that act as the local housing authority (ONS, May 2026). The registration service asks whether each property needs an HMO, additional or selective licence, and for the licence numbers (draft Schedule 3, paragraph 10), so check your council first. Mandatory HMO licensing applies everywhere; these schemes are on top.

On 28 October 2026 we found 21 councils in London with selective licensing and 25 with additional HMO licensing, in force or designated to start.

Before 14 October 2027

£65 a property a year (fee). Upload the gas safety record, EICR and EPC (full list) within 28 days or the entry becomes inactive (draft reg 11(2)(a)); renew anything that lapses before 14 October 2027 first. Up to £7,000 if you miss it (penalties).

Questions

When do landlords in London have to register?

The requirement starts on 15 July 2027 and the deadline is 14 October 2027. The service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

Which councils in London have selective licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Barking and Dagenham, Bexley, Brent, Croydon, Ealing, Greenwich, Hackney, Hammersmith and Fulham, Haringey, Harrow, Havering, Islington, Lambeth, Lewisham, Merton, Newham, Redbridge, Southwark, Waltham Forest, Wandsworth and Westminster. Most schemes cover only some wards, so check the council's map for your street.

Which councils in London have additional HMO licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Barking and Dagenham, Brent, Camden, Croydon, Ealing, Greenwich, Hackney, Hammersmith and Fulham, Haringey, Harrow, Havering, Hillingdon, Hounslow, Kensington and Chelsea, Lambeth, Lewisham, Merton, Newham, Redbridge, Southwark, Sutton, Tower Hamlets, Waltham Forest, Wandsworth and Westminster.
