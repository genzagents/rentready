Landlord database registration in the South East: deadline 14 June 2027

If your rental is in the South East, you must register it on the government's Register your rental property service (the landlord database, or PRS Database) by 14 June 2027. The requirement for the South East starts on 15 March 2027; the service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier. The region goes by the property's address, not yours. The South East covers Brighton and Hove, Southampton, Portsmouth, Reading, Oxford, Milton Keynes, Canterbury, Guildford, Slough and Maidstone.

Councils in the South East and their licensing schemes

The South East has 64 local councils that act as the local housing authority (ONS, May 2026). The registration service asks whether each property needs an HMO, additional or selective licence, and for the licence numbers (draft Schedule 3, paragraph 10), so check your council first. Mandatory HMO licensing applies everywhere; these schemes are on top.

Before 14 June 2027

£65 a property a year (fee). Upload the gas safety record, EICR and EPC (full list) within 28 days or the entry becomes inactive (draft reg 11(2)(a)); renew anything that lapses before 14 June 2027 first. Up to £7,000 if you miss it (penalties).

Questions

When do landlords in the South East have to register?

The requirement starts on 15 March 2027 and the deadline is 14 June 2027. The service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

Which councils in the South East have selective licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Brighton and Hove, Medway and Oxford. Most schemes cover only some wards, so check the council's map for your street.

Which councils in the South East have additional HMO licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Arun, Brighton and Hove, Medway, Oxford, Portsmouth, Reading, Southampton and Woking.
