Landlord database registration in the East Midlands: deadline 14 May 2027

Written by GenZAgents, reviewed 7 November 2026. Sources below.

If your rental is in the East Midlands, you must register it on the government's Register your rental property service (the landlord database, or PRS Database) by 14 May 2027. The requirement for the East Midlands starts on 15 February 2027; the service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier. The region goes by the property's address, not yours. The East Midlands covers Nottingham, Leicester, Derby, Northampton, Lincoln, Chesterfield and Loughborough.

Has your council told landlords yet?

Landlords in the East Midlands register third, from 15 February 2027, so on 7 November 2026 we read every council's own landlord pages. 18 of 35 mention the landlord database at all, and only 3 give the dates (the 15 December 2026 opening or the 14 May 2027 deadline). Don't wait for a letter from the council: the duty to register is yours, and the government's service is the only place to do it.

Councils in the East Midlands and their licensing schemes

The East Midlands has 35 local councils that act as the local housing authority (ONS, May 2026). The registration service asks whether each property needs an HMO, additional or selective licence, and for the licence numbers (draft Schedule 3, paragraph 10), so check your council first. Mandatory HMO licensing applies everywhere; these schemes are on top.

On 28 October 2026 we found 7 councils in the East Midlands with selective licensing and 3 with additional HMO licensing, in force or designated to start.

EPCs that run out before 14 May 2027

An EPC lasts ten years, and the service asks for a valid one. By our count of the government's EPC register (full data story), about 12,200 rental EPCs in the East Midlands reach ten years between 1 November 2026 and 14 May 2027, 5,700 of them inside the region's own registration window. Far more follow: 142,600 reach ten years in 2028 to 2030. These are certificates, not homes, and include social lets.

Questions

When do landlords in the East Midlands have to register?

The requirement starts on 15 February 2027 and the deadline is 14 May 2027. The service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

Has my council in the East Midlands told landlords about the landlord database?

On 7 November 2026, 18 of the 35 councils in the East Midlands mentioned it on their own website (Boston, Charnwood, Derby, Derbyshire Dales, East Lindsey, Erewash, Gedling, Harborough, Hinckley and Bosworth, Leicester, Lincoln, North East Derbyshire, North Northamptonshire, Oadby and Wigston, Rushcliffe, South Holland, South Kesteven and West Northamptonshire), and 3 gave the dates. Registration is the landlord's duty either way.

Which councils in the East Midlands have selective licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Ashfield, Charnwood, Gedling, Leicester, Mansfield, Nottingham and Oadby and Wigston. Most schemes cover only some wards, so check the council's map for your street.

Which councils in the East Midlands have additional HMO licensing?

As of 28 October 2026: Charnwood, Nottingham and West Northamptonshire.

How many rental EPCs in the East Midlands expire before the landlord database deadline?

About 12,200 reach ten years between 1 November 2026 and 14 May 2027, most in Nottingham (2,400), by our count of the government's EPC register.
