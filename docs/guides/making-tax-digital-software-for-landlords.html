    How to choose, in four checks

    1. UK property and the tax return. Quarterly updates are only half the job. For 2026-27 your software also sends the final declaration (the tax return) by 31 January 2028. A product without it means switching or paying twice next winter.
    2. Everything else on your return. MTD software files your whole return, so it must handle your wages, pensions, dividends and any property you sold. "In development" means the company has promised HMRC it will be ready for the 2026-27 return; it is not there yet.
    3. Records or spreadsheet. If you already keep a good spreadsheet, bridging software sends its figures to HMRC. Otherwise pick software that keeps the records, ideally with a bank feed.
    4. The real price. HMRC only says whether a free version exists. Free versions often cap properties or transactions, so read the company's pricing page before you start, and check the product is still listed on HMRC's finder.

    Then sign up for MTD if HMRC has not done it for you, authorise the software in your HMRC account, and send the update due by 7 November 2026 (it covers 6 April to 5 October). Need the figures? Work out your quarterly update from a bank statement.

    Questions

    Is there free Making Tax Digital software for landlords?

    Yes. Of the 165 products HMRC recognises, 22 have a free version and can send UK property quarterly updates and the tax return. Check what the free version includes on the company's site: some limit the number of properties or transactions, and HMRC does not vet prices.

    Can I keep using my spreadsheet for Making Tax Digital?

    Yes, with bridging software, which reads your spreadsheet and sends the figures to HMRC. 55 products that handle UK property and the tax return offer bridging. The spreadsheet itself must be your digital record, with each rent and expense line, and figures must move to the software digitally, not retyped.

    Does my MTD software have to file my tax return?

    Yes. From the 2026-27 tax year, due 31 January 2028, anyone in Making Tax Digital files the tax return through compatible software, not HMRC's online form. 47 of the 136 products that send UK property updates do not list the tax return, so with one of them you would need a second product next January.

    What if I have wages, a pension or sold a property as well as rent?

    Your MTD software files your whole tax return, so it must handle every other income on it. Tick those items in the finder. Products marked in development have promised HMRC they will be ready for the 2026-27 return.
