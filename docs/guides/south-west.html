Landlord database registration in South West: deadline 14 November 2027

If your rental is in South West, you must register it on the government's Register your rental property service by 14 November 2027. The requirement for South West starts on 15 August 2027; the service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

The region goes by the property's address, not yours. South West covers Bristol, Plymouth, Exeter, Bath, Bournemouth, Swindon, Gloucester, Cheltenham and Truro.

What it costs

£65 per property per year; five properties cost £325. Renewal is every 12 months: the renewal date is the last day of the 12 months from your first entry, one date for the whole portfolio (draft regulation 10); later properties pay pro rata. The fee explained.

What you upload for each property

  • Latest gas safety record (CP12) and its issue date, if the property has gas
  • EICR (or EIC if no EICR yet) and its expiry date
  • Latest EPC, and any MEES exemption; if the EPC has lapsed, the start date of the current tenancy

Check the dates now: a certificate that lapses before 14 November 2027 has to be renewed first. A gas safety record lasts 12 months, an EICR five years, an EPC ten years. Guides: gas safety, EICR, EPC and rating.

What you will be asked

  • Landlord: name, date of birth, residential address, phone, email (companies: entity type, registered address, Companies House number or all directors)
  • Property: address, ownership type, freeholder or superior landlord, dwelling type, bedrooms, currently let?
  • Tenancy: rent, bills included?, payment frequency, occupiers, households, HMO licence status, furnishing
  • Each joint landlord needs their own landlord entry linked to the one property entry; an agent cannot register for you

Everything the service asks.

If you miss 14 November 2027

  • Up to £7,000 civil penalty for letting or marketing without an active entry, omitting the reference from adverts, or not updating within 28 days
  • Up to £40,000 for the section 92 offences: knowingly or recklessly giving false or misleading information, continuing a breach 28 days after a penalty, or a different breach within five years of one; rent repayment orders can also apply
  • No possession order from the court while the landlord or dwelling entry is inactive, except on Ground 7A or Ground 14 (section 90)

Questions

When do landlords in South West have to register?

The requirement starts on 15 August 2027 and the deadline is 14 November 2027. The service opens to every landlord in England on 15 December 2026, so you can register earlier.

How much is the landlord database fee in South West?

£65 per property per year, the same in every region.

Does the deadline go by where I live or where the property is?

Where the property is. A landlord living elsewhere with a property in this region meets this region's deadline.
