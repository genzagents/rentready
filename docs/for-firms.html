Why landlords will need you this winter

From 15 December 2026 every private landlord in England must register each let property on the landlord database (PRS Database), region by region, and upload a current gas safety record, EICR and EPC with each one. The West Midlands registers first, by 14 March 2027; London is last for most, by 14 October 2027. Our analysis of the government's EPC tables found about 243,000 rental EPCs reach ten years old before their region's deadline. Landlords who check early book early.

What your customers see

  • A box at the top of the checker: "Shared with you by" your business name, phone and website.
  • Under each certificate you cover that has expired or runs out before their deadline: "Book with" your business, with your number.
  • In the calendar file they download: your name and number on every expiry date for the certificates you cover, so the reminders 60 and 14 days before say who to call.
  • A note that rentready is independent and has not checked your firm, with a link to the Gas Safe Register for gas work.
